# Expense Reimbursement Procedure — India

**Document ID:** FIN-POL-007 | **Revision:** 2.0 | **Status:** CURRENT
**Effective:** 1 April 2026 | **Supersedes:** Revision 1.6
**Applies to:** India region only | **Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** Regional Finance Controller, India

## 1. Scope
This procedure governs reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees based in the India region. Employees in EMEA must follow FIN-POL-008 instead. This document does not apply to capital purchases.

## 2. Submission Deadline
Claims must be submitted through the expense portal within thirty days of the expense date. Claims older than sixty days are rejected and require a written exception from the Regional Finance Controller.

## 3. Receipt Requirements
Itemised receipts are mandatory for any single expense of INR 500 or more. Expenses below that threshold may be claimed with a self-declaration. Digital receipts are acceptable if legible and unaltered.

## 4. Approval Limits
Reporting managers may approve claims up to INR 50,000 per employee per month. Claims above that limit require countersignature from the Regional Finance Controller. Claims above INR 200,000 additionally require Group Finance approval.

## 5. Payment Timeline
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle, provided approval is complete five working days before the payroll cut-off. Claims approved after that cut-off are paid in the following cycle.

## 6. Travel Advances
Travel advances up to INR 25,000 may be requested for approved business travel. Advances must be settled within fifteen days of return, and unsettled advances are recovered from payroll.
