# Expense Reimbursement Procedure — EMEA

**Document ID:** FIN-POL-008 | **Revision:** 1.1 | **Status:** CURRENT
**Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Supersedes:** Revision 1.0
**Applies to:** EMEA region only | **Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** Regional Finance Controller, EMEA

## 1. Scope
This procedure governs reimbursement of business expenses incurred by employees based in the EMEA region. Employees in India must follow FIN-POL-007 instead. Value added tax treatment is covered separately by the local tax addendum.

## 2. Submission Deadline
Claims must be submitted through the expense portal within forty-five days of the expense date. Claims older than ninety days are rejected without exception.

## 3. Receipt Requirements
Itemised receipts are mandatory for any single expense of EUR 25 or more. VAT receipts are required where the employee intends the company to reclaim input tax. Photographs of receipts are acceptable.

## 4. Approval Limits
Reporting managers may approve claims up to EUR 1,000 per employee per month. Claims above that limit require countersignature from the Regional Finance Controller. Claims above EUR 5,000 additionally require Group Finance approval.

## 5. Payment Timeline
Approved claims are paid by bank transfer within ten working days of final approval, independent of the payroll cycle.

## 6. Travel Advances
Travel advances are not offered in the EMEA region. Employees should use the corporate card programme described in the EMEA finance handbook.
