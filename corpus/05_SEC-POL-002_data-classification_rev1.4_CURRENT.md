# Data Classification and Device Standards

**Document ID:** SEC-POL-002 | **Revision:** 1.4 | **Status:** CURRENT
**Effective:** 1 February 2026 | **Supersedes:** Revision 1.3
**Applies to:** Global — all employees, contractors and interns | **Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer

## 1. Classification Levels
Company information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Public information may be shared freely. Internal information may be shared within the company. Confidential information requires a named business need. Restricted information requires written approval from the data owner and is logged.

## 2. Customer Data
Customer data is Confidential at minimum and Restricted where it contains personal or payment information. Customer data must never be stored on personal devices, personal cloud accounts, or unmanaged removable media.

## 3. Device Standards
Any device used for company work must have full disk encryption enabled, a screen lock of five minutes or less, an automatic security update channel, and remote wipe capability. Devices that cannot meet these standards must not be used for company work.

## 4. Contractors and Interns
Contractors and interns are covered by this policy in full. They are not eligible for remote work arrangements under HR-POL-014 and must work from a company office or an approved client site.

## 5. Incident Reporting
Suspected data loss, device theft, or unauthorised access must be reported to the security team within twenty-four hours of discovery. Late reporting is itself a policy violation.

## 6. Review Cycle
This policy is reviewed annually. The next scheduled review is February 2027.
