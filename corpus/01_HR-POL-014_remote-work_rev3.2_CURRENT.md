# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-014 | **Revision:** 3.2 | **Status:** CURRENT
**Effective:** 1 March 2026 | **Supersedes:** Revision 2.8 (14 August 2024)
**Applies to:** All full-time employees, India and EMEA | **Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** Head of People Operations

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the conditions under which employees may perform their duties from a location other than a designated company office. It replaces revision 2.8 in its entirety. Where this document and any earlier revision conflict, this document governs.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees who have completed six months of continuous service are eligible to request remote work. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible until that plan formally concludes. Contractors and interns are out of scope and are covered by SEC-POL-002 instead.

## 3. Approval Process
Requests must be submitted to the reporting manager at least ten working days in advance. The manager must respond within five working days. Where a manager does not respond in that window, the request escalates automatically to the regional HR partner, who must decide within a further three working days.

## 4. Equipment and Security
The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. Employees must not store customer data on personal devices. Any device used for company work must have full disk encryption enabled and a screen lock set to five minutes or less. Device standards are defined in SEC-POL-002.

## 5. Internet Stipend
A monthly stipend of INR 2,500 is available for internet costs in the India region. The EMEA region stipend is EUR 40 per month. Claims are submitted through the expense portal by the fifth day of the following month. Regional reimbursement procedures are defined in FIN-POL-007 and FIN-POL-008.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Head of People Operations and are granted for a maximum of ninety days. Exception requests must state a business justification and an end date.
