# Parental Leave Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-021 | **Revision:** 1.0 | **Status:** APPROVED — NOT YET EFFECTIVE
**Effective:** 1 January 2027 | **Supersedes:** None — new policy
**Applies to:** All full-time employees, India and EMEA | **Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** Head of People Operations

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes parental leave entitlements. It takes effect on 1 January 2027. Before that date no company-wide parental leave entitlement exists beyond statutory minimums, and enquiries should be directed to the regional HR partner.

## 2. Primary Caregiver Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to twenty-six weeks of paid leave in the India region and eighteen weeks in the EMEA region. Leave must commence within twelve months of birth or placement.

## 3. Secondary Caregiver Leave
Secondary caregivers are entitled to four weeks of paid leave in both regions, which may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first year.

## 4. Notice Requirements
Employees should give at least eight weeks notice where circumstances allow. Shorter notice is accepted for premature birth or emergency placement.

## 5. Return to Work
Employees returning from parental leave may request a phased return over four weeks at full pay. Requests are submitted to the reporting manager and the regional HR partner jointly.
