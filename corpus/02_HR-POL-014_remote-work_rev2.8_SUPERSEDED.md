# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-014 | **Revision:** 2.8 | **Status:** SUPERSEDED
**Effective:** 14 August 2024 | **Superseded by:** Revision 3.2 (1 March 2026)
**Applies to:** All full-time employees, India and EMEA | **Classification:** Internal
**Owner:** Head of People Operations

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the conditions under which employees may perform their duties from a location other than a designated company office. This revision is retained for audit reference only and must not be relied upon for current decisions.

## 2. Eligibility
Employees who have completed twelve months of continuous service are eligible to request remote work. Managers may waive the service requirement at their own discretion.

## 3. Approval Process
Requests must be submitted to the reporting manager at least five working days in advance. There is no defined response time for managers and no automatic escalation path.

## 4. Equipment and Security
The company provides a laptop. External monitors are not provided. Full disk encryption is recommended but not mandatory. Screen lock should be set to fifteen minutes.

## 5. Internet Stipend
A monthly stipend of INR 1,800 is available for internet costs in the India region. The EMEA region stipend is EUR 30 per month. Claims are submitted by the tenth day of the following month.

## 6. Exceptions
Exceptions require approval from the reporting manager and are granted for a maximum of one hundred and eighty days.
